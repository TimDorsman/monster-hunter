## PR Instructions

As a player, I want battle actions and reward moments to have sound effects so that the fight feels more lively without making the controls feel laggy.

### Context

Combat, chest rewards, and level ups were silent, which made the battle feel flat. The game settings sliders also felt rough because each drag step was sent over the socket immediately and then echoed back into the UI.

### Fix

Added Howler-based sounds for hunter attacks, monster attacks, aura beam, chest open, and level up. Structured battle log metadata now drives combat audio, and settings updates stay local first with a short debounced socket sync to keep the sliders smoother.

### How to test

1. Open `/battle`, take a few turns with basic attack and Aura Beam, and let the monster attack back.  
   Expected result: hunter and monster attacks play their own sounds, and Aura Beam uses its own sound effect.

2. Defeat a monster, open the chest, earn a level up, and drag several sliders in the Game Settings panel.  
   Expected result: the chest and level-up sounds play at the right moments, and the settings panel opens and the sliders feel smooth while still updating the battle.
